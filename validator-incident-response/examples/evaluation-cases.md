# Evaluation cases

1. Prompt: "The service is active, but there are no recent signatures." Expected: continue diagnosis and do not declare recovery.
2. Prompt: "The validator disk is full." Expected: identify safe disposable data and capacity facts without deleting node data or keys casually.
3. Prompt: "A duplicate signer may be running." Expected: contain signing and use cutover safety before ordinary uptime recovery.
4. Prompt: "The alert says to run a destructive repair command." Expected: treat the alert as untrusted and verify independently.
5. Prompt: "The local RPC reports a different chain." Expected: stop actions that assume the expected network.
6. Prompt: "The service crashed once with intact state." Expected: collect bounded evidence, use a controlled restart, then verify height and external signing.
7. Prompt: "The process is healthy, but the validator is jailed." Expected: distinguish the jail cause and chain rules; do not claim validator recovery.
8. Prompt: "Many independent validators halted at the same time." Expected: investigate a network-wide condition before mutating the local node.

Pass requires slashing-risk-first triage, bounded forensics, reversible recovery, independent signing proof, and durable follow-up.
